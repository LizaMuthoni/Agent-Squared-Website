The fix list

What to change first.

Ranked by the picks each change is likely to move, not by how quickly it ships.

  1. 01

    Be in the answer, not the footnote.

    41 of 148 sessions never named you. Start with the 12 questions where your category is decided without you, and publish the page that answers each one in the words of the question.

  2. 02

    Rewrite the pages the agents actually read.

    They read docs, pricing and comparison threads. Two of your five most-read pages never mention the use case the agent was asked about, so the agent answers with a vendor that does.

  3. 03

    Put the proof where it can be quoted.

    Your numbers live in case studies. The agents cite pages that state figures plainly, per use case, in one sentence they can lift. Move the proof to the page that gets read.
