Why teams run one

Why this beats a visibility dashboard.

Mention tracking tells you that your name appeared. A category study tells you what the agent decided, why, and which page was holding you back.

Answers, not impressions

The metric is the one your buyer's agent actually uses: who gets named first. Reach and share of voice do not survive that hand-off.

Recorded, not guessed

Every session is logged with question, phrasing, agent, pick order and pages read. You can check our work line by line.

Repeatable

Same question set, same three phrasings, every run, so the next study shows movement instead of noise.
