      Rewrite the DAM product page to win the read

      Evidence

      Up to 16 of 144 sessions (11%) touch this one page; DAM overall is 24 sessions where Brand X is currently 2nd.

      Action

      Move the DAM entity name and the buyer's verdict into the first screen of Brand X's DAM product page instead of the current general media-platform framing. This is the single most-read page in the whole study, opened in 16 of 144 sessions, and it still sent the recommendation to one rival three times, to a second rival twice, and to nine other DAM vendors once each while Brand X won only twice. Fold in the stronger framing already working on Brand X's DAM solution page, which converts better on a smaller sample, and add a short comparison table naming two other vendors directly.
